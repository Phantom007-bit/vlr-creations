VLR Policies & FAQ

Everything you need to know before you order.

What is your Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ)?
Our Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ) is 25-50pcs depending on the specific item and customization requirements for your brand.
What is the estimated turnaround time?
Our standard turnaround time is 1 week from payment depending on order size and complexity.
What is your Return and Refund Policy?
Due to the custom nature of our garments, we have a strict No refunds policy. However, we stand by our quality. If any errors are from production, pieces will be returned and replaced.
How do I send my design or logo files?
To ensure the highest print quality, please send your high-resolution artwork (PNG, PDF, or Vector) directly to our team via WhatsApp immediately after submitting your cart.
